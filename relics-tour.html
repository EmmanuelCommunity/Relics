Making the Heart of Jesus known and loved

The Emmanuel Community will continue the tour of St. Margaret Mary Alacoque’s Major Relics in the United States once the Knights of Columbus’ tour has finished.

Between 1673 and 1675, St. Margaret Mary, a sister of the Monastery of the Visitation in Paray-le-Monial, received apparitions of Jesus, who made known to her the immense love of his Sacred Heart. He lamented that he is not loved in return, and he asked her to spread the devotion to the Sacred Heart throughout the world.

The traveling reliquary contains her two clavicles, two of her ribs, and a small piece of her brain. Relics are physical reminders that heaven is obtainable for us. When we venerate them, we thank God for our spiritual family and ask the saints to intercede for us.

We invite pilgrims on a “Journey to the Heart of Jesus” as they come to venerate her. She helps those who come to discover the secrets of his Heart.

The reliquary and its home

The major relics are usually kept and venerated in the Chapel of Apparitions in Paray-le-Monial, France. In 1986 the Sanctuary was entrusted to the Emmanuel Community, which welcomes thousands of pilgrims of the Sacred Heart every year. This tour brings the Sanctuary to the places the relics visit.

The Emmanuel Community is an international Catholic community recognized by the Holy See as a public association of the faithful. More at emmanuelcommunity.com/relicstour.
